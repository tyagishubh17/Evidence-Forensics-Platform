Forensic Integrity Protocol

Evidence integrity is established through NIST-standard SHA-256 cryptographic hashing at the moment of intake:

1
Non-Destructive Storage
Original file is stored under a server-generated UUID to eliminate collisions and path-traversal attacks.
2
Immediate Hash Calculation
Python hashlib computes the 64-character SHA-256 digest in 64 KB chunks.
3
Immutable Audit Baseline
Recorded hash is stored alongside evidence metadata in SQLite. Any byte alteration triggers a MISMATCH.
Section 63 BSA / Legal Context: Cryptographic hashes provide tamper-evident verification that an electronic record has remained unaltered since reception.
